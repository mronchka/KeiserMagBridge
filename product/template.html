How it works.

Matched to your bike in minutes.

M3 Bridge learns your pedal stroke and every gear position, then lines its numbers up with your Keiser console. What you see on the bike is what Zwift sees.

  1. Pedal for ten seconds.It learns your cadence.
  2. Shift through every gear.Each one is matched to your console.
