Calibrate gears

You will record 25 lever positions. Hold the lever still at each one.

  1. Point 1: gear lever all the way down.
  2. Points 2 to 24: move the lever up until the console first shows the next gear, then stop pedalling.
  3. Point 25: gear lever all the way up.

With auto-capture on, each point is taken once the reading has held steady for about 2 seconds. Otherwise tap CAPTURE.

Calibration is done while pedalling. Keep pedalling the whole time; the console shows the gear while you pedal.

  1. First the bridge learns your crank: pedal steadily at 60–80 rpm for 5 to 15 seconds. It beeps twice when done. Check the rpm matches the console.
  2. Point 1: gear lever all the way down.
  3. Points 2 to 24: move the lever up until the console first shows the next gear, then hold it there.
  4. Point 25: gear lever all the way up.

With auto-capture on, each point is taken once 3 pedal turns in a row agree. Otherwise tap CAPTURE.

Every finished calibration is saved on this phone automatically.
